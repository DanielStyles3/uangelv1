Our purpose

More than food. Food with a purpose.

U Angel Foods is a social enterprise by Ecclesia Nation. Here is what happens when you buy from us.

  1. You buy

    Order food, groceries, market produce, cakes, drinks or catering in the U Angel Foods app.

  2. Every purchase funds our outreach

    The profit from what you buy supports our outreach programs.

  3. Someone is cared for

    We get to know individuals in need, understand their stories, and provide for them.

What makes us different? Every item you purchase contributes directly to feeding the less privileged.

A letter from Ecclesia Nation

Let Us Build

Dear Sir/Ma,

The world is going through one of the most difficult economic periods in its history. Every day, billions of people struggle to afford even a single decent meal. Families are forced to make impossible choices between feeding their children, paying rent, buying medicine, or simply surviving another day.

Across our cities, we see widows carrying unimaginable burdens, orphaned children without adequate care, people experiencing homelessness, and many individuals trying to rebuild their lives after addiction, or other life-altering challenges like child labour and sex trafficking.

As the Body of CHRIST, we have always responded with compassion. Yet, while generosity is essential, the scale of today's challenges also calls for sustainable systems that can provide continuous support for years to come.

It is with this vision that Ecclesia Nation is excited to introduce U Angel Foods.

U Angel Foods is a social enterprise established with the goal of transforming lives. As a social enterprise we are at the intersection of a for-profit and a non-profit. Our mission is simple: to build profitable businesses whose profits are used to cater for the less privileged, support evangelism, establish soup kitchens, and create employment opportunities within the Body of CHRIST.

Rather than depending solely on occasional acts of benevolence, we know the Church can build sustainable economic systems that continually generate resources for ministry, charity, and community development. Businesses are engines that finance the work of GOD while restoring dignity to those in need.

We warmly invite you to support this initiative by purchasing our products. Your purchases will feed people, create jobs, improve communities, strengthen Christian businesses, and build a lasting legacy of compassion for generations to come.

Thank you for partnering with us as we seek to serve CHRIST by serving people. Together, we can build a stronger Church, and a better world.

Yours faithfully,

Ecclesia Nation
