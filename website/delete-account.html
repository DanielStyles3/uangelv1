Your account

Delete your U Angel account

This applies to the U Angel Foods and U Angel Affiliates apps by U Angel Foods. Both apps use the same account, so deleting it removes it from both.

Delete it in the app (instant)

  1. Open U Angel Foods or U Angel Affiliates and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

Or ask us to delete it

If you can’t open the app, email us from the address you signed up with. We’ll delete your account within 7 days and reply to confirm.

Email a deletion request WhatsApp us

What happens to your data

Deleted

  • Your sign-in and profile (name, email, phone)
  • Saved delivery details
  • Catering and cake requests
  • Your affiliate account, referral code, referral links, commission and payout bank details

Kept, unlinked from your account

  • Past order records (items, amounts, dates and the delivery details on each order), for our accounts and tax records, as required by law
  • Payment records held by Paystack under their own policy

Commission that hasn’t been paid out when you delete your affiliate account is lost, so withdraw it first. Read our Privacy Policy for more.
