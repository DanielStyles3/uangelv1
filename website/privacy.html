Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy explains what information U Angel Foods (“we”, “us”) collects when you use the U Angel Foods app, the U Angel Affiliates app and uangelfoods.com, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We are based in Lagos, Nigeria.

In short: we collect only what we need to take, deliver and support your orders and to run the affiliate programme. We never sell your information.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number and password (stored securely; we can’t see it).
  • Delivery details: the addresses and notes you enter so we can deliver your order.
  • Orders and requests: what you order, prices, delivery choices, Christmas giving messages, and catering or cake enquiries (event dates, guest numbers, notes).
  • Payments: card and bank payments are handled by Paystack. We receive the payment result and reference, never your full card details.
  • Affiliate programme: if you join, your referral code, the people who signed up with it, order totals and commission amounts, and the bank account details you give us for payouts. Affiliates see only the total spent and commission for each person they referred, never what was ordered.
  • Messages to us: what you send through WhatsApp, phone, email or the app’s enquiry forms.
  • Crash reports: if the U Angel Foods app crashes, technical details about the error and your device (model, system version) are sent to Sentry so we can fix it.

We do not use your location, contacts, camera or microphone, and we do not show advertising.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and confirm your email.
  • To take payment, prepare and deliver your orders, and keep you updated about them.
  • To respond to enquiries and provide customer support.
  • To run the affiliate programme: link customers to the affiliate who referred them, calculate commission and pay it out.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, and to keep records the law requires.
  • To fix problems and improve the apps.

Who we share it with

  • Supabase, which hosts our database and sign-in.
  • Paystack, which processes payments.
  • Sentry, which receives crash reports.
  • Our delivery staff, who see your name, phone number and delivery address for your order.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

These providers only use your information to provide their service to us. Your data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account is open. When you delete your account, your profile, saved details, requests and affiliate account are deleted. Past order records (including the delivery details on each order) are kept, unlinked from your account, because we need them for our accounts and tax records.

Your choices and rights

  • You can view and edit your details in the app at any time.
  • You can delete your account in either app (Profile → Delete account) or by asking us.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct it, by contacting us below. These rights are protected by the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Children

Our apps are not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
